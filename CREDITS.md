# Credits

Deepstone is built on the work of the artists and musicians below. Thank you. The same list is in
the game, in the Credits tab of the Character screen.

The code is MIT licensed (see [LICENSE](LICENSE)). The art and audio below are not part of that
licence: each pack keeps its own terms, linked next to it. Some files are kept out of the public
repository because their terms do not allow publishing the raw files, and are added when the game
is built.

## Art

- **16x16 Dungeon Tileset** by 0x72 (https://0x72.itch.io/16x16-dungeon-tileset)
  License: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Used for: dungeon tiles, doors, chests, NPCs, decorative sprites.
- **DungeonTileset II** by 0x72 (https://0x72.itch.io/dungeontileset-ii)
  License: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Used for: the tiles of the hand-drawn dungeon rooms, chests and the door leaf.
  Modified: the block of vertical wall sprites on the master sheet (x 0-96, y 128-192) was moved up
  by 8 px so every wall piece sits on the same 16 px grid phase. No pixel was added or removed.
  A surplus sheet was added to it, so the two wall sets can mix in one room. It holds the four wall
  banners cut out on a transparent background, the lava and water wall fountains reworked from the
  Dark Dungeon Tileset below (the water recolored with this tileset's water colors), and four half
  floors cut from its plain floor tile.
- **16x16 Dark Dungeon Tileset** by Kosinaz (https://kosinaz.itch.io/16x16-dark-dungeon-tileset)
  License: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/). Based on 0x72's DungeonTileset II.
  Used for: the tiles of the dark dungeon rooms.
- **Pixel Crawler** (Free Pack) by Anokolisa
  (https://anokolisa.itch.io/free-pixel-art-asset-pack-topdown-tileset-rpg-16x16-sprites)
  License: the pack's own terms. Free for personal and commercial projects, credit is appreciated, the
  art may be altered, and it may not be sold or marketed as a final product by others.
  Used for: the enemy sprites.
- **Kyrise's 16x16 RPG Icon Pack** by Kyrise (https://kyrise.itch.io/kyrises-free-16x16-rpg-icon-pack)
  License: [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/), attribution required.
  Used for: class icons, the currency icon and several item icons.
  Modified: the icons are cut out of the original sheet.
- **4SS Platform** by MoDsama (https://modsama.itch.io/4ssplatform)
  License: the pack's own terms. Free for personal and commercial projects, adaptation allowed, credit
  required, the art may not be resold or distributed on its own.
  Used for: the warrior, archer and mage sprites.
  Modified: cut into animations per class and mirrored for the left-facing set.
- **RPG Weapons, Armour and Mage Packs** by CaseIRL (https://caseirl.itch.io/)
  License: the packs' own terms. Free for personal and commercial use, the packs may not be
  redistributed or resold as they are. Attribution appreciated.
  Used for: weapon, armor and some accessory icons.
- **UI Pack**, **UI Pack (RPG Expansion)** and **Game Icons** by Kenney (https://kenney.nl)
  License: [CC0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
  Used for: the combat action buttons and the checkmark (UI Pack), the health and resource bars
  (UI Pack (RPG Expansion)), and the settings and lock icons (Game Icons).
- **Tabler Icons** by Paweł Kuna (https://tabler.io/icons)
  License: [MIT](https://github.com/tabler/tabler-icons/blob/master/LICENSE).
  Copyright (c) 2020-2023 Paweł Kuna.
  Used for: the room type, marker and arrow glyphs of the dungeon map, as placeholders until a
  game-style icon set replaces them.
- **Sanctuary halo**, made for Deepstone
  Hand-drawn by the game's author, then recolored and rescaled with the help of an AI tool
  (Claude Cowork).
  Used for: the halo of the Sanctuary, the last room of the dungeon.
